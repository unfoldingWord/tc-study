# es-419-x-yaapi pilot (20 Nov 2026)

Design: `handoff/mini-servant/yaapi-diseno.md` (BT Servant workspace). Tools: `packages/resource-pipeline/cli/fetch-yaapi.ts`, `build-yaapi-patch-v3.ts`, `data/yaapi-versions.json`.

## What was built
- Snapshot of two yaapi versions of Spanish, fetched 20 Nov 2026 (one chapter per call): SPAPDDPT (Palabra de Dios para ti, CC BY 4.0, readers / literal) and ONBV-AST (Biblica Open Nueva Biblia Viva, CC BY-SA 4.0, readers / dynamic).
- Patch `es-419-x-yaapi` revision 1, signed with the staging key, built against the published `es-419` (what it has text of: rut neh est jon tit 3jn) and common revision 6.
- 34 books with SPAPDDPT, 32 with ONBV-AST; 34 book segments (897 KB), catalog 4.4 KB. Not uploaded.
- Books: jos jdg ezr pro lam amo oba hab zep hag, the NT except act and 3jn/tit (base has them), 2co and rev only in SPAPDDPT.

## Books left out (26 of the 60 without text) and why
The rule is exact: every verse of the original, none else. yaapi lacks verses where its numbering differs from the original's (and some it simply does not return): gen exo lev num deu 1sa 2sa 1ki 2ki 1ch 2ch job psa ecc sng isa jer ezk dan hos jol mic nam zec mal act. Examples: Numbers has 16 chapters in yaapi (Num 17 "Verse not found"), Joel stops after chapter 2, Mal 4 is absent. The Door43 es-419 texts use English numbering (Jonah 1 has 17 verses), the original segment uses Hebrew numbering, so a versification map would be needed for these books, and yaapi's own holes would remain. Nothing is remapped. 2co (ONBV) and rev (ONBV) miss one verse each and act 19:40.

## Checks
- Both readers (TS, Python) agree on all 35 files (catalog + 34 segments).

- Unit tests: builder (5), client merge (5), catalog reader audience/style (TS and Python).

## Format
No change to the book segment (`texts` without `align`). Optional `audience` and `style` on resource entries (catalog.cddl, catalog.md, both readers). The patch rule in catalog.md now allows unaligned texts.
