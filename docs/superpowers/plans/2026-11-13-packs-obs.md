# Open Bible Stories in the language packs (format 3), T-071 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. The controller follows `.claude/lp3-controller.md` and dispatches `lp3-implementer`, `lp3-reviewer` and `lp3-runner`.

**Goal:** The parts build reads OBS and its helps from the fingerprinted zips, the format 3 packs carry the `obs` unit, and the shared library answers passage → stories, search over stories, key term / article / person → stories, and a whole story; the try page shows it.

**Architecture:** Three layers, as the design says (approach A). (1) `resource-pipeline`: discovery picks one OBS owner per language, `ObsLoader` reads stories from the release zip, a converter turns old markdown OBS notes and questions into the TSV rows, and OBS gets its own parts unit so the shared unit (TW, TA) keeps its fingerprint; the refusals go and the workflow gets an `obs` input (off). (2) `language-pack-v3`: the `obs` unit already exists (`catalog.ts:97`); it gains `needs: ['map']`, and the build reports its size. (3) `language-pack-client`: two new queries (`stories`, `story`), an `obs` field on `concept` and `books`, and OBS rows in the search index. The try page (another repo) shows them.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Bun 1.3.3 (`bun test`), JSZip (already used by the loaders), the existing `language-pack-v3` codecs.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-13-packs-obs-design.md` (approved 2026-11-13, with its "Calls made after the builder check"). Format: `packages/language-pack-v3/spec/` (`catalog.md`, `counts.md`, `cddl/obs.cddl`, `ids.md`). The design is the authority; where this plan and the design disagree, the design wins and the controller records a ruling.

## Choices this plan makes (left to the builder by the design)

1. **Passage → stories is a new query, `stories(ref)`,** not a field on `passage`. `passage` only takes verse forms (`parseRef`: `"gen 1"` is `E_ARG`), and the design's test is "Genesis 1". `stories` takes `"gen 1"` (a whole chapter) and every form `passage` takes. `passage` is untouched, which keeps the client diff small for C2 and C5.
2. **A whole story is `story(n)`.**
3. **Key term / article / person → stories** is an `obs` field on `concept` (per story) and on `books` (one cell, `BookCell`, counted like a book).
4. **Search** keeps one index: OBS rows have `book: 'obs'`, `ref: 'NN:FF'` (`'NN:0'` for a title), and a new kind `'title'`. Kinds `text`, `note`, `question` are reused for frames, OBS notes and OBS questions. OBS word links are not indexed (Bible links are, but an OBS link carries no words of its own).
5. **Markdown helps → rows:** one row per `#` heading of a frame file `content/NN/FF.md`: heading = Quote (TN) or Question (TQ), the text under it = Note (TN) or Response (TQ), reference `N:F`. `content/NN/00.md`, `intro.md` or `title.md` are frame 0. Task 3 checks this against the real `hi_obs-tn` and `hi_obs-tq` zips before writing code and records the final rule in the ledger.
6. **OBS parts unit:** `buildLanguageParts` builds OBS (stories and helps) as a unit of its own, `obs`, next to the shared unit. Turning OBS on then leaves the shared unit's fingerprint, and its carry from `--previous`, exactly as with OBS off (design call 2).
7. **Owner rule (design "Which resources" and call 3):** the stories come from the catalog's `prod` OBS of the Bible pack's owner, else from the language's `prod` OBS of any owner (more than one: the most recent `prod` release; a tie: the owner name in code-point order). Each OBS help (TN, TQ, TWL) comes from the owner of the chosen stories, else by the same fallback. Nothing in OBS needs `tc-ready`. If the picks for en, es-419, id, hi or ar differ from the table in the design, the controller stops and asks Abel (scope).
